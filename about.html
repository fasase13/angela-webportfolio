Professional Profile & Passion

Talisay City, Negros Occidental

Hello! I am Angela M. Oñate, an Information Systems student at Carlos Hilado Memorial State University (CHMSU) in Talisay City, Negros Occidental.

My passion lies at the intersection of business requirements and intuitive human experiences. While technology provides the backbone for modern organizations, it is thoughtful documentation, clear information hierarchy, and user-friendly UI/UX design that empower people to thrive.

During my On-the-Job Training (OJT) at the Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO) in Talisay City Hall, I handled front-line document processing, citizen records verification, data encoding, and inter-office logistics. Witnessing firsthand how citizens and staff navigate complex paperwork reinforced my commitment to designing seamless, digital-first workflows.
